How to get a good reading
  1. Hold still against something fixed to the ship: rest the phone's edge on the window sill or frame, or lay it flat on a table. Holding it in the air adds your own hand movement.
  2. Give it at least 3 minutes per stretch. The sway is slow (about 14 s), so short samples mislead.
  3. Pick the stretch you're on (it switches automatically by the clock), then press Copy summary and send it to me.
